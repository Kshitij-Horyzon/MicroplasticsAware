🔬 Definition

WHAT ARE MICROPLASTICS?

Microplastics are plastic fragments smaller than 5mm — some invisible to the naked eye. They never fully break down; they only get smaller, entering water supplies, food chains, and ultimately our bodies.

🔵
PRIMARY

Intentionally manufactured at micro-scale. Found in cosmetic microbeads, toothpaste, industrial abrasives, and synthetic clothing fibres. 35% of ocean microplastics come from synthetic textiles alone.

Microbeads Fibres Pellets
🔴
SECONDARY

Form when larger plastic items break down under UV light, wind, waves, and physical abrasion. Plastic bottles, bags, and packaging fragment into billions of particles over decades.

HEALTH EFFECTS UNDER RESEARCH
Inflammation
Microplastics trigger immune responses in lungs and gut tissue. IARC is evaluating carcinogenicity as of 2024.
Endocrine Disruption
Plastic additives (BPA, phthalates) act as endocrine disruptors, interfering with hormone signalling.
Neurotoxicity
Nanoplastics have been found crossing the blood-brain barrier in animal studies (Nature Neuroscience, 2023).
